# Site brief: the real Shaolin Temple

**Inspiration only** since the project became a game (see `docs/game-design.md`): the game's Shaolin follows this central axis loosely and takes liberties with terrain and scale. Every number carries a source. `[conflict]` means sources disagree, `[unverified]` means one weak source or recall only. Resolve these from the UNESCO nomination file (1305rev) and maps before the number goes into `site data`.

Gathered 15 Nov 2026 from web search summaries; most source sites could not be opened directly from the build environment.

## Compound

- Core compound (常住院 Chángzhù Yuàn): seven progressions on one axis, rising **22.61 m** front to back in steps. ([Zhengzhou Daily, 2008](https://zzrb.zynews.cn/page/1/2008-09/12/06/2008091206_pdf.PDF))
- Size: about 300 m north–south × 120 m east–west `[conflict]` (another source gives 57,600 m²). ([maigoo](https://m.maigoo.com/citiao/67559.html))
- Sits at the foot of Wuru Peak (五乳峰), facing roughly south towards Shaoshi Mountain. Exact bearing `[unverified]`.
- Coordinates about 34.507 N, 112.935 E `[unverified]`.
- 1928 fire destroyed the Hall of Heavenly Kings, Mahavira Hall, Dharma Hall and Bell Tower. Mountain Gate, Lixue Pavilion and Thousand Buddha Hall survived. ([source](https://www.epochtimes.com/gb/10/5/9/n2903056.htm))
- UNESCO World Heritage 2010: "Architectural Complex of Shaolin Temple (Kernel Compound, Chuzu Temple, Pagoda Forest)". ([maps](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1305/maps))

## Outside the compound

- **塔林 Pagoda Forest**: 300–400 m west; tomb pagodas dated 791–1803; count 228–248 `[conflict]`; area 14,000–21,000 m² `[conflict]`.
- **初祖庵 Chuzu Temple**: about 1 km north-west; main hall 1125, 3 × 3 bays.
- **达摩洞 Bodhidharma Cave**: on Wuru Peak above Chuzu Temple.

## Visitor route (the tour's default path)

Mountain Gate → stele path and ginkgo → Hall of Heavenly Kings → Mahavira Hall with Bell and Drum towers → Sutra Library → Abbot's Room → Lixue Pavilion → Thousand Buddha Hall with Baiyi and Dizang halls → west lane → Pagoda Forest. The real visit takes 1 to 1.5 hours.
